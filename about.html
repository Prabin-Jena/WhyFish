METHODOLOGY & GROUNDING

Stockfish knows what. Whyfish explains why.

Whyfish takes a chess position and uses Stockfish's real calculations to generate human-readable tactical explanations strictly grounded in the engine's verified telemetry.

01 // EVIDENCE

ENGINE EVIDENCE

Stockfish analyzes the supplied position and produces candidate lines, evaluations, and relevant squares with mathematical rigor.

02 // GROUNDING

GROUNDED EXPLANATION

The structured engine output is supplied to the language model with explicit constraints on the coordinates and lines it can reference.

03 // VALIDATION

MECHANICAL VALIDATION

Generated moves and evidence references are checked against the structured engine output before they reach the interface.

ARCHITECTURE // PIPELINE

How Whyfish works

  1. 01
    Position A FEN position is supplied by the user or chosen from curated scenarios.
  2. 02
    Stockfish The engine calculates the position, principal variations, and candidate evaluations.
  3. 03
    Grounding Structured engine evidence is supplied to the explanation model with bounded context.
  4. 04
    Mechanical Validation Generated moves and evidence squares are mechanically checked against engine output.
  5. 05
    Grounded Explanation The validated explanation is presented with interactive chessboard square telemetry.
